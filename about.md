**[ChemPal Tutor](https://chempal.pro/)** is an AI-powered chemistry learning companion for
high school, AP, and college students.

Rather than handing over a final answer, it works through problems step by step — identifying
the concept, choosing the right equation, substituting values, checking units and significant
figures, and explaining what the result means.

## What it offers

- Step-by-step solutions with explanations that adapt to your level
- Lewis structures, VSEPR geometry, resonance, and 2D/3D molecular rendering
- An interactive Lewis structure drawing tool with hints and feedback
- AI-generated flashcards, practice problems, and quizzes
- Answer evaluation that explains *why* something is wrong
- Progress tracking with streaks and experience points
- 16 languages, with voice playback tuned for chemistry notation

This blog covers product updates, chemistry study guides, and tips for learning more
effectively with an AI tutor.
